The Team

The people behind Curatores

Cūrātōrēs means “those who take care of something.” These are the people who take care of Curatores.

Conditores

The founders

  • Co-founder

    Aiden Cheng

    I have been taking Latin for six to seven years. My teachers have taught me not only a language, but different ways to think. Latin has improved my ability to deconstruct sentence structures and has improved my vocabulary significantly. Seeing the Latin department slowly diminish and some of my Latin teachers getting laid off has inspired me to start this cause. I want to bring both the legacy of Latin as well as its practical uses in the modern world.

  • Co-founder

    Devan Mody

    While many others decided to go with Spanish or French I chose Latin. Latin is a language which I have been learning for around 7 years and every year I take it makes me like the language even more. But unfortunately I have seen several school districts remove the Latin program and even districts that still have it are getting way less attention from both students and the schools themselves. I wanted to change this and help bring this great language back up.

  • Co-founder

    Girik Jaganish

    I have been taking Latin for five years. I could still remember my first Latin class. I was sitting in front of my computer screen, my eyes glued to the very words that enlighten me today. I saw the grammar systems and new vocabulary I could discover, and I could not help but shed a tear. I was deeply in love with the language. My life changed at the end of ninth grade. Seeing my old teachers lose their jobs was truly heartbreaking to me. I vowed to fight for the language that had carried me to the top these past few years. From now on, I hold my head high, for Julius Caesar and the Romans.

  • Co-founder

    Syed Aayan

    Over my 6 years of studying Latin, my classes usually always had the same dedicated people in it. This class allowed me to learn without stressing me out like many other classes do. Latin became more than a class, it became a community which helped me build lasting connections with fellow students and my teachers. My friends in my Latin classes helped make it one of my most enjoyable classes. I want to bring this sense of belonging and community to more people.

    Ayyan Zeeshan

    I designed and built this website to give the case for Latin a home worthy of it. Its look comes from the wall paintings of Pompeii: deep red, parchment and gold. Underneath are the parts you use: flip cards, a case explorer, a quiz, progress that saves as you go, and a consultation form that reaches the team directly. New chapters will be added as the course grows.
