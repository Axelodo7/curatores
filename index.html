About Curatores

Looking after a language you already use.

Curatores teaches Latin from the words up. The course is short and practical: each chapter teaches a set of words, shows how their endings change, traces them into modern English, and ends with a quiz so you know what stuck.

Every chapter, four steps

  1. Step 1

    Vocabulary

    Learn the words on flashcards: greetings, nouns, adjectives and verbs.

  2. Step 2

    Cases

    See how a word’s ending changes its job in the sentence.

  3. Step 3

    Etymology

    Trace the words into medicine, law and paleontology.

  4. Step 4

    Quiz

    Fifteen questions with instant feedback to check what stuck.
